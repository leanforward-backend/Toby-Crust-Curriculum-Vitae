TickyTick · Personal product

Local-first task manager with realtime sync

Tasks, calendar planning, habits and focus sessions in one codebase that runs in the browser, installs as a PWA and builds to an Android APK. IndexedDB is the source of truth, so every interaction is instant and works offline; a sync engine pushes changed rows to Supabase Postgres and pulls remote changes back over a realtime websocket, with Row Level Security scoping every row to its user. Includes a Gemini function-calling assistant whose proposed changes are reviewed before they are saved.
